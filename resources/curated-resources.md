## Resource entry standard

Every new entry should include:
- Resource name and canonical official URL
- Category and concise description
- Demo or playground URL, when verified
- Video tutorial URL, when available
- Pricing or free-tier notes, when verified
- License information for libraries or downloadable assets

Keep one canonical entry per website. Do not invent demo links, pricing, or licensing details.
