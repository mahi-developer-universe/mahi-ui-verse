# Responsible Disclosure

We welcome good-faith reports that help protect project users and
contributors.

Please:
- Share findings privately with the maintainer.
- Provide reproducible evidence and a reasonable impact assessment.
- Avoid accessing data that does not belong to you.
- Avoid disrupting services or destroying data.
- Allow a reasonable remediation period before public disclosure.

Do not include real credentials, personal information, or sensitive
customer data in reports.
